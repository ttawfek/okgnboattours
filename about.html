About OKGN Lake Tours

on the water

The premium, small-group alternative to the party-boat scene: considered, relaxed, and quietly luxurious.

Our Approach

Earned, not endured

Some days on the lake are meant to be earned, not endured. OKGN Lake Tours offers unhurried, private charters for up to 12 guests — no driving, no crowds, no rental-counter hassle. Just a comfortable boat, a knowledgeable local captain, and the shoreline of the North Okanagan unfolding at an easy pace.

Why OKGN Lake Tours

North Okanagan local

Your Captain

Local at the helm

OKGN Lake Tours is owned and operated by Levi Ralston, a Vernon-based certified captain (SVOP). Every charter runs on a Transport Canada registered commercial vessel, fully insured and licensed in the City of Vernon — with insider knowledge of the lake's best coves, viewpoints, and quiet corners.
