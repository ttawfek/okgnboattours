Privacy Notice

What we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Private captained pontoon charters · Okanagan Lake · Vernon, BC
Effective date: May 1, 2026

At OKGN Lake Tours, we respect your privacy. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the information we collect when you inquire about, book, or take a charter with us.

Who we are

OKGN Lake Tours, operated by Levi Ralston, Vernon, BC. If you have any questions about your privacy or this notice, contact Levi Ralston at levi@okgnlaketours.com or 250-503-7594.

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, and emergency-contact details
  • Booking details (dates, group size, occasion, preferences)
  • Payment information — processed securely by our payment provider (Square). We do not store your full card number.
  • Waiver and safety-orientation records you sign before a charter
  • Photos or video from a charter, only where you have consented
  • Messages and correspondence with us

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes
  • Take and manage your booking, and process deposits and payments
  • Communicate with you about your trip (confirmations, reminders, receipts)
  • Keep required safety and waiver records
  • Follow up after your trip and, if you're open to it, invite a review
  • Send occasional updates or offers (you can opt out anytime)
  • Meet our legal, tax, and insurance obligations

We use your information only for these purposes, and we do not sell your personal information.

Consent

By contacting us, booking, or paying, you consent to our collecting and using your information as described here. You can withdraw consent or unsubscribe from marketing at any time by contacting us — though we may need to keep certain records (such as booking, payment, and safety records) as required by law.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business and handle it on our behalf, such as:

  • Square — payment processing and customer records
  • Google — our Business Profile and reviews
  • Email and marketing tools we use to communicate with you

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect safety. These providers have their own privacy policies, and some (including Square and Google) may store data on servers outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

How we protect and keep it

We keep your information secure and only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example, tax, insurance, and safety records), after which we securely delete it.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to stop using it for marketing. Contact Levi Ralston at levi@okgnlaketours.com or 250-503-7594 and we'll be glad to help.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version will be posted on our website.
