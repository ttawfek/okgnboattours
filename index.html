OKGN Lake Tours

A calmer kind of day on the water

Some days on the lake are meant to be earned, not endured. OKGN Lake Tours offers unhurried, private charters for up to 12 guests — no driving, no crowds, no rental-counter hassle. Just a comfortable boat, a knowledgeable local captain, and the shoreline of the North Okanagan unfolding at an easy pace. It's the premium, small-group alternative to the party-boat scene: considered, relaxed, and quietly luxurious.
